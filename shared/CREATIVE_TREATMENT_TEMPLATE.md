# Creative Treatment Template

## Purpose

The Creative Treatment defines the project at Development level and prepares an ECD Greenlight decision.

Apply `CREATIVE_TREATMENT_PRESENTATION.md` for visible hierarchy and `STAGE_SCOPED_INPUT_REGISTER.md` for cross-stage material.

## Template rules

- The first two sections are mandatory.
- Supplemental fields may be compressed or omitted when irrelevant.
- Development meaning may be stated; final sequence, copy, visual execution, and production remain outside the Treatment.
- Deferred notices preserve later-stage inputs without interpreting them as completed craft decisions.
- The response that submits the Treatment ends at the Greenlight request.

## QA

Before submission confirm:

- the core idea is clear without reading metadata;
- the narrative is coherent and positive;
- angle, proposition, and audience consequence agree;
- evidence, rights, and reference boundaries match the intended promise;
- later-stage information is registered and Deferred;
- no final Script or visual system is embedded in the Treatment;
- Greenlight scope and next stage are explicit.
