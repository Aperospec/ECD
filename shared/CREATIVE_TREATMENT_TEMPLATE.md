# Creative Treatment Template

## Purpose

The Creative Treatment defines the project at Development level and prepares an ECD Greenlight decision.

The complete Treatment remains a connected creative proposition. Internal control records support it without replacing it.

Apply:

- `CREATIVE_TREATMENT_PRESENTATION.md`
- `ARTIFACT_DECISION_CONTRACT.md`
- `PRODUCER_CONTROL_LOOP.md`
- `STAGE_SCOPED_INPUT_REGISTER.md`

## Template rules

- The first two sections are mandatory and lead the ECD-facing proposal.
- Governing logic, audience consequence, and relevant boundaries must be recoverable from the complete Treatment.
- Optional project-specific decisions are added only when the project requires them.
- Do not force every project into agency, narrative, transformation, or character fields.
- Supplemental fields may be compressed when irrelevant.
- Development may define meaning; final sequence, Frame Scripts, copy, visual execution, and production remain outside the Treatment.
- Deferred notices preserve later-stage inputs without interpreting them as completed craft decisions.
- Development returns the Treatment to Creative Producer for review.
- Only a Producer Cleared Treatment may be released for Greenlight.
- The response submitting the Treatment ends at the Greenlight request.

## QA

Before specialist return confirm:

- the core idea is clear without metadata;
- the narrative is coherent and positive;
- angle, proposition, governing logic, and audience consequence agree;
- evidence, rights, reference, and semantic boundaries match the intended promise;
- authoritative upstream decisions and delta are traceable;
- later-stage information is registered and Deferred;
- no final Script or visual system is embedded;
- the artifact is ready for Producer Review.
