# Acceptance Record

## Purpose

The Acceptance Record distinguishes professional handoff, ECD alignment of representative decisions, and final ECD acceptance.

It prevents a preliminary route, anchor, or technical completion from being mistaken for final delivery.

## Acceptance Rules

- Creative Producer may mark a package ready for review but may not mark it Final Accepted on the ECD's behalf.
- Visual Direction Alignment does not accept anchors or final images.
- Anchor / Design Alignment does not accept every later frame automatically.
- Technical QA does not resolve a subjective visual rejection.
- Final acceptance does not authorize publication unless publication authority is included or already explicit.
- A requested revision must be routed to the first failed object, not automatically to Production Artist.
- If final assets are ugly because the Concept Route or anchors were wrong, reopen those objects even when production files are technically correct.
