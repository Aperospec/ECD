# Social Editorial Production Model

## Purpose

This document defines the shared production language for ECD. It intentionally describes only the studio skeleton. Role-specific methods belong inside each Skill and will be developed separately.

## Core Production Chain

The canonical professional artifacts are:

```text
Source Material
  ↓
Research / Material Brief
  ↓
Creative Treatment
  ↓
Creative Script
  ↓
Art Direction
  ↓
Production Execution
  ↓
Final Assets
```

These are professional handoff objects, not mandatory paperwork. Use only the artifact depth required by the project.

## Artifact Ownership

- **Research / Material Brief** — Research Editor
- **Creative Treatment** — Editorial Strategist
- **Creative Script** — Editorial Director
- **Art Direction** — Art Director
- **Final Production Assets** — Production Artist
- **Production Brief / Decision State / Acceptance Record** — Creative Producer

## Creative Treatment

The Creative Treatment defines what the piece is about and why it should exist. It should establish the editorial premise, selected angle, audience relevance, intended understanding, and important claim boundaries without prematurely designing the sequence or visual composition.

## Creative Script

The Creative Script defines how the piece communicates. Depending on format it may contain content structure, sequence, card/page roles, storyboard descriptions, on-screen copy, supporting copy, captions, transitions, and ending logic.

It may describe visual intent when necessary to communicate meaning, but final art direction remains with the Art Director.

## Art Direction

Art Direction defines how the approved Creative Script becomes a coherent Social Editorial visual experience: visual concept, hierarchy, typography, composition, image direction, image–type relationships, cover/body-page grammar, visual rhythm, and production intent.

## Production Execution

Production Execution realizes approved art direction faithfully and deterministically. It handles implementation details and technical QA without becoming a new creative authority.

## Dynamic Depth

Not every project needs every artifact at maximum depth.

Examples:

- A raw public post may require research before strategy.
- A user-supplied, already verified idea may begin at editorial strategy.
- A fully approved Creative Treatment may go directly to Editorial Director.
- An approved Creative Script may begin with Art Director.
- Existing approved art direction may go directly to Production Artist.

The Creative Producer chooses the shortest valid route.
