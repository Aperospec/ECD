# Greenlight Record

## Purpose

Greenlight identifies the Creative Treatment the Executive Creative Director accepted as the authoritative Development premise and records its decision basis, Producer clearance, and authorization evidence.

## Object rule

Greenlight attaches to an identifiable, Producer Cleared Treatment version or an explicitly supplied equivalent.

A general instruction to begin work is Development authorization, not acceptance of an unseen premise.

## Normal turn boundary

```text
Development self-QA
→ Producer Review
→ Producer Cleared Treatment
→ Creative Producer presents the complete Treatment and recommendation
→ requests Greenlight and stops
→ ECD responds in a later turn
```

A concise ECD response is valid when its conversational referent is the identified Treatment submitted for decision.

## Valid authorization forms

- explicit ECD response to a complete presented Treatment;
- identifiable prior Greenlight tied to a recoverable Treatment, Producer Review, and decision record;
- stage-aware instruction identifying supplied material as the final Treatment and authorizing Editorial development.

## Scope

Greenlight makes authoritative only the Development decisions recorded in the identified Treatment and Accepted Development Decisions.

It normally authorizes Editorial development next.

It does not accept final sequence, Frame Scripts, copy, visual direction, Storyboard, Design Comp, Art Direction, or final assets.

## Accepted Development Decisions

Record a compact, project-appropriate decision set rather than forcing every project into the same worksheet.

Mandatory:

- **Creative core** — what the project fundamentally proposes or reframes;
- **Governing logic or relationship** — how the idea holds together;
- **Intended audience consequence** — what the audience should understand, feel, notice, imagine, or reconsider;
- **Boundaries** — factual, speculative, semantic, rights, reference, claim, and excluded-direction limits that matter.

Add project-specific decisions only when required, such as agency, evidence structure, temporal logic, world logic, product mechanism, character relationship, or other governing conditions.

These decisions support inheritance and QA. The complete Treatment narrative remains authoritative.

## Conditional Greenlight

Use only when a non-governing condition can be resolved without changing the accepted Development decisions.

A condition affecting the creative core, governing logic, intended audience consequence, essential evidence, rights basis, or boundary remains a Development blocker.

## Reopening

Reopen Development when a proposed delta changes or removes an accepted Development decision.

Creative Producer identifies the earliest affected decision, preserves unaffected authority, and presents a new complete ECD decision object.

## Gate QA

A claimed Greenlight is valid when:

- Treatment and version are identified;
- Development self-QA and Producer Review passed;
- the ECD reviewed a complete decision object;
- Producer recommendation and relevant delta were visible;
- authorization evidence is traceable;
- accepted decisions and boundaries are recorded;
- scope is stage-local;
- active and Deferred capabilities are explicit;
- no internal role granted ECD authority on the user's behalf.
