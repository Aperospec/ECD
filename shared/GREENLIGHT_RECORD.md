# Greenlight Record

## Purpose

Greenlight identifies the Creative Treatment the Executive Creative Director has accepted as the authoritative production premise and records the authorization evidence.

## Object rule

Greenlight attaches to an identifiable Treatment version or an explicitly supplied equivalent. A general instruction to begin work is Development authorization, not acceptance of an unseen premise.

## Normal turn boundary

For raw or unresolved input:

```text
Creative Producer presents the Treatment
→ states decision scope and next authorized stage
→ requests Greenlight and stops
→ ECD responds in a later turn
```

A concise response is valid when its conversational referent is the identified Treatment submitted for decision.

## Valid authorization forms

- explicit ECD response to a presented Treatment;
- identifiable prior Greenlight tied to a recoverable Treatment and decision record;
- stage-aware instruction identifying supplied material as the final Treatment and authorizing the named next stage.

## Scope

Greenlight makes authoritative only the Development-level decisions recorded in the identified Treatment. It normally authorizes Editorial development next.

It does not accept final sequence, copy, visual direction, Storyboard, Design Comp, or final assets.

## Pre-Greenlight scope

Before Greenlight, valid work includes intake, research, source inspection, reference-role and rights analysis, premise development, Treatment drafting, and decision preparation.

Editorial scripting, final audience copy, visual-development artifacts, broad image production, layout, and export remain inactive.

## Conditional Greenlight

Use only when a non-premise condition can be resolved without changing the governing project. A condition affecting premise, central claim, essential rights, or audience takeaway remains a Development blocker.

## Reopening

Reopen Development when a proposed change materially alters the accepted premise, angle, proposition, public position, factual or speculative boundary, essential rights basis, reference mandate, or audience consequence.

## Gate QA

A claimed Greenlight is valid when:

- Treatment and version are identified;
- the ECD reviewed a complete decision object;
- authorization evidence is traceable;
- scope is stage-local;
- active and Deferred inputs are explicit;
- no internal role granted ECD authority on the user's behalf.
