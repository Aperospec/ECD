# Development Function

## Purpose

Development turns a raw or unresolved ECD input into a clear, testable, and potentially Greenlightable Social Editorial project.

It is a backstage professional capability coordinated by Creative Producer rather than an independent user-facing personality.

Development may receive a brief containing later-stage material. It must use only the **Active Development projections** from `STAGE_SCOPED_INPUT_REGISTER.md` and preserve later-stage projections as Deferred.

## Core Question

> Is there a project worth making here, and if so, what exactly is it?

## Inputs

Development may begin from:

- a sudden idea or sentence;
- a public post, article, repository, product, paper, news item, or data point;
- an image, screenshot, or visual reference;
- rough notes or partial copy;
- an existing concept or Treatment;
- a product or commercial opportunity;
- a correction that changes the premise of earlier work;
- a complete natural-language brief spanning Development, Editorial, Visual, and Production concerns.

Creative Producer first determines intended use, valid existing work, complexity profile, evidence obligation, and stage projections.

A request to make or begin a deliverable is still a Development input when no Creative Treatment has yet been presented or explicitly identified as already approved.

## Cross-Stage Input Boundary

Before Development begins, Creative Producer must preserve material raw inputs in the Stage-Scoped Input Register.

Development receives:

- global Deliverable Contract inputs required to understand the project;
- Active Development projections;
- evidence / rights / reference-boundary questions needed for Treatment formation.

Development does **not** receive later-stage projections as executable instructions.

### Cross-stage statements

One source statement may produce a Development projection and a Deferred later-stage projection.

Example:

```text
ECD: “建筑非常宏大，人很小。”

Development projection:
The work should make the scale and breadth of a person's life perceptible.

Deferred Art Direction projection:
Monumental architecture with a small human figure.
```

Development may use the first to clarify the project's meaning. It must not lock the second as the final visual solution.

### User downstream Hard Constraints

When the ECD explicitly makes a later-stage visual or production choice non-negotiable, preserve it as a Deferred Hard Constraint.

Do not discard it. Do not treat it as a completed downstream artifact. It becomes active when the responsible stage starts.

## Development Capabilities

Use only what the project requires.

### 1. Premise Diagnosis

Determine:

- what is actually being proposed;
- whether the input is a topic, source, visual impulse, product claim, or project premise;
- what is interesting, weak, generic, derivative, unsupported, or unresolved;
- what must be true for a project to deserve Production.

### 2. Research / Verification / Validation

Use `RESEARCH_FUNCTION.md` according to the selected evidence obligation.

Research constrains truth, rights, and promise. It does not choose the creative premise by itself or activate visual craft work.

### 3. Reference Intelligence — Development Scope

Use `REFERENCE_POLICY.md` and the Stage-Scoped Input Register.

During Development, clarify:

- what reference class applies;
- why the ECD supplied it;
- whether direct use is authorized;
- rights / attribution / license conditions;
- prohibited transfer;
- any Development-level semantic consequence.

Do not perform detailed palette, lighting, materiality, camera, typography, or composition extraction solely because a visual reference is available.

If the ECD says a reference is liked for “色调、画风”, record that intended use faithfully and defer detailed extraction to Art Director after Visual activation.

### 4. Editorial Development

May:

- distinguish topic from angle;
- develop insight;
- test audience relevance;
- explore materially different angles;
- develop the core proposition;
- challenge unsupported assumptions;
- define reality, imagination, evidence, and claim boundaries;
- define what the work must not imply or become;
- draft and revise the Creative Treatment.

Development may recommend Greenlight, reframing, further validation, pause, or rejection.

It must not convert a user Editorial Seed into a final sequence during Development.

## Development Loop

```text
ECD input
↓
Creative Producer intake
↓
Stage-Scoped Input Register
├─ Active Development projections
├─ Deferred Editorial projections
├─ Deferred Visual projections
└─ Deferred Production projections
↓
Development functions as required
↓
Creative Producer synthesis
↓
Creative Treatment
+ optional Deferred Input Notice
↓
ECD Greenlight request
↓
Hard stop awaiting decision
```

Development may loop without becoming Production.

## Primary Artifact — Creative Treatment

Use `CREATIVE_TREATMENT_TEMPLATE.md`.

The Treatment should establish:

- what the project is;
- the creative / editorial premise;
- why it deserves to exist;
- selected angle and core proposition;
- audience relevance and intended experience;
- relevant facts and uncertainty;
- reality / imagination boundary;
- material public-claim limit;
- reference-use, rights, attribution, and asset boundaries at Development level;
- what the project must preserve semantically and must not falsely imply or become.

The Treatment should not pretend that later-stage user instructions are already final professional decisions.

## Deferred Input Notice

When the raw brief contains important later-stage information, Creative Producer may show a concise notice outside the Treatment body:

```text
Deferred inputs recorded, not part of this Greenlight:
- Editorial: [source-faithful user directions]
- Visual: [source-faithful visual preferences / reference intents]
- Production: [when relevant]
```

This notice exists to prove the information was captured, not to interpret or approve it prematurely.

## Development Boundary

Development must not prematurely produce:

- final page / card sequence;
- final audience-facing on-screen or companion copy;
- actual Storyboard / Visual Sequence Board;
- detailed reference palette / lighting / camera / typography / composition extraction;
- Representative Design Comp;
- final visual composition or typography system;
- Art Direction Package;
- production assets.

A Treatment may note likely communication behavior or material deliverable consequences without becoming the Creative Script.

### Tool boundary

Before Greenlight, Development may inspect supplied images and references, perform research, and create text-based Development artifacts.

It must not invoke image generation, image editing, rendering, layout, Figma production, export, or other visual-production tools for the deliverable. Research-oriented image discovery or inspection is allowed only as evidence or reference analysis.

## Greenlight Readiness

Development may recommend Greenlight when:

- the premise is coherent and non-generic enough to guide Production;
- ECD intent and the proposed project are aligned at the Development level;
- evidence obligation has been satisfied to the required depth;
- material reference, rights, and attribution boundaries are known;
- later-stage inputs are registered and Deferred rather than lost or prematurely executed;
- no unresolved issue would fundamentally change what the project is;
- the Deliverable Contract is compatible with the premise.

Only the ECD can Greenlight the project.

For raw or unresolved input, Creative Producer must present the Treatment, state that Greenlight authorizes Creative Script development next, request the decision, and stop.

Complexity, detailed source material, and generic production instructions do not replace this decision.

A direct transition without a new presentation turn is valid only for a traceable prior Greenlight or an explicit stage-aware instruction identifying supplied material as the final / approved Treatment and authorizing Production from it. Apply `GREENLIGHT_RECORD.md`.

## Treatment Presentation Contract

The user-visible Development result should contain, at the depth appropriate to the project:

- the proposed Creative Treatment;
- principal Development-level reference-use and rights boundaries;
- material factual, speculative, or claim boundaries;
- optionally, a source-faithful Deferred Input Notice;
- the recommendation;
- what Greenlight will make authoritative;
- **authorized next stage if accepted: Creative Script development**;
- what remains unaccepted after Greenlight;
- one concise Greenlight request.

End the response there. Do not append a Creative Script, page-by-page sequence, Storyboard proposal, detailed visual reference extraction, image prompt, visual mockup, or generated asset.

## Quality Gate

Before presenting the Treatment:

- separate verified fact, source claim, inference, and speculation;
- make the angle and proposition explicit;
- state the audience consequence rather than generic relevance;
- identify what is original about the Treatment;
- record what the work must not become;
- confirm all material raw inputs are registered;
- confirm later-stage inputs remain Deferred;
- confirm Assistant Inference is not masquerading as user authority;
- remove premature page plans, copy, palette, lighting, composition, or layout;
- recommend a direction rather than presenting undifferentiated options when professional judgment can resolve them;
- confirm that the next action is an ECD Greenlight decision, not visual Production.
