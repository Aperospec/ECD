# Development Function

## Purpose

Development turns raw or unresolved input into a clear, testable, Greenlightable project.

It is a backstage professional capability coordinated by Creative Producer.

## Core question

> Is there a project worth making, and what exactly is it?

## Inputs

Development may begin from an idea, source, product, image, reference, rough copy, data point, existing concept, or premise-level correction.

Creative Producer first establishes intended use, stage projections, complexity, evidence obligation, authoritative existing work, active capabilities, assignment, and acceptance criteria.

## Capabilities

Use only what the project requires.

### Premise diagnosis

Determine:

- what is actually being proposed;
- what is specific, valuable, unresolved, generic, derivative, unsupported, or contradictory;
- what governing logic or relationship makes the project coherent;
- what the project must establish to deserve production;
- which assumptions require evidence or ECD authority.

### Research / Verification / Validation

Apply `RESEARCH_FUNCTION.md` according to the intended promise.

Research constrains truth, rights, provenance, and feasibility. It does not choose the creative premise by itself.

### Reference intelligence

Apply `REFERENCE_POLICY.md` to record intended role, rights, provenance, and Development-level consequence.

Detailed visual extraction remains Deferred until Visual activation.

### Creative development

Develop:

- insight;
- selected angle;
- core proposition;
- governing logic or relationship;
- audience relevance and intended consequence;
- factual, speculative, rights, reference, claim, semantic, and excluded-direction boundaries;
- Creative Treatment.

Development may recommend Greenlight, revision, validation, pause, or rejection to Creative Producer.

## Cross-stage discipline

Development receives only Global / Deliverable and Active Development projections.

Editorial, Visual, and Production projections and capabilities remain Deferred.

A cross-stage source statement may contribute Development meaning while retaining separate later-stage craft consequences. Acceptance of Development meaning does not accept downstream execution.

## Artifact decision discipline

Development records:

- authoritative upstream artifacts;
- inherited decisions;
- decisions introduced by the Treatment;
- open or Deferred decisions;
- relevant delta;
- conflicts and authority consequences.

Apply `ARTIFACT_DECISION_CONTRACT.md`.

## Creative Treatment

The Treatment establishes:

- One-Sentence Creative Core;
- coherent creative narrative;
- premise, angle, proposition, governing logic, and audience consequence;
- why the project deserves to exist;
- material factual, speculative, rights, reference, claim, and semantic boundaries;
- project-specific governing decisions only when required;
- what remains Deferred.

It does not contain final Editorial Architecture, Frame Scripts, audience copy, Storyboard, visual system, image production, layout, or export.

## Specialist self-QA and return

Before returning the Treatment to Creative Producer, Development confirms:

- source fact, inference, and speculation are distinguishable;
- the governing idea leads the presentation;
- the logic and audience consequence are specific;
- evidence and rights obligations are sufficient;
- inherited decisions and delta are traceable;
- cross-stage inputs remain inactive;
- no open issue would fundamentally change the project;
- the artifact is complete enough for Producer Review.

Return:

- Treatment and version;
- Development self-QA;
- Artifact Decision Contract;
- evidence, rights, and reference state;
- open issues and limitations;
- recommended Producer disposition.

Development does not release directly to the ECD.

## Producer Review and Greenlight

Creative Producer independently verifies the Treatment.

Deficient work is returned internally.

Only a Producer Cleared Treatment may be presented to the ECD with:

- complete creative proposition;
- relevant boundaries;
- Producer recommendation;
- relevant delta and feedback closure;
- exact Greenlight scope;
- one decision request.

The release turn ends at the Greenlight request.

## Development boundary

Before Greenlight, valid work includes intake, research, source inspection, reference-role analysis, premise development, Treatment drafting, specialist self-QA, and Producer Review.

Formal Script, final audience copy, visual development artifacts, image production, layout, and export remain inactive.

## Quality gate

Development self-QA passes when:

- the project can be stated clearly and positively;
- the governing logic or relationship is coherent;
- the intended audience consequence is concrete;
- evidence and rights boundaries match the promise;
- no authoritative upstream decision was silently changed;
- later-stage inputs remain preserved and inactive;
- the Treatment is a synthesis, not a worksheet dump;
- the next internal action is Producer Review.
