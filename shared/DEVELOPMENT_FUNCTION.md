# Development Function

## Purpose

Development turns raw or unresolved input into a clear, testable, Greenlightable project.

It is a backstage capability coordinated by Creative Producer.

## Core question

> Is there a project worth making, and what exactly is it?

## Inputs

Development may begin from an idea, source, product, image, reference, rough copy, data point, existing concept, or premise-level correction.

Creative Producer first establishes intended use, stage projections, complexity, evidence obligation, and valid existing work.

## Capabilities

Use only what the project requires.

### Premise diagnosis

Determine:

- what is actually being proposed;
- what is specific, valuable, unresolved, generic, derivative, unsupported, or contradictory;
- what the project must establish to deserve production;
- which assumptions require evidence or ECD authority.

### Research / Verification / Validation

Apply `RESEARCH_FUNCTION.md` according to the intended promise. Research constrains truth, rights, provenance, and feasibility; it does not choose the creative premise by itself.

### Reference intelligence

Apply `REFERENCE_POLICY.md` to record intended role, rights, provenance, and Development-level consequence. Detailed visual extraction remains Deferred until Visual activation.

### Editorial development

Develop:

- insight;
- selected angle;
- core proposition;
- audience relevance and intended consequence;
- factual, speculative, rights, and claim boundaries;
- semantic guardrails;
- Creative Treatment.

Development may recommend Greenlight, revision, validation, pause, or rejection.

## Cross-stage discipline

Development receives Active Development projections and Global / Deliverable information. Later-stage Editorial, Visual, and Production projections remain Deferred.

A cross-stage source statement may contribute Development meaning while retaining separate later-stage craft consequences. Acceptance of the Development meaning does not accept the later execution.

## Creative Treatment

The Treatment establishes:

- One-Sentence Creative Core;
- coherent creative narrative;
- premise, angle, proposition, and audience consequence;
- why the project deserves to exist;
- material factual, speculative, rights, reference, and claim boundaries;
- what the project must preserve semantically;
- what remains Deferred.

It does not contain the final sequence, final copy deck, Storyboard, visual system, or production assets.

Apply:

- `CREATIVE_TREATMENT_PRESENTATION.md`
- `CREATIVE_TREATMENT_TEMPLATE.md`

## Greenlight readiness

A Treatment is ready to submit when:

- the project can be stated clearly and positively;
- the angle and proposition are specific;
- the audience consequence is concrete;
- evidence and rights obligations are sufficient for the intended promise;
- cross-stage inputs are registered and correctly Deferred;
- no open issue would fundamentally change the project;
- the ECD-facing presentation is complete.

Creative Producer presents the Treatment, states the decision scope and next authorized stage, requests Greenlight, and stops at the gate.

## Development boundary

Before Greenlight, valid work includes research, source inspection, reference-role analysis, premise development, Treatment drafting, and decision preparation.

Formal Script, final audience copy, visual development artifacts, image production, layout, and export remain inactive.

## Quality gate

Before presenting the Treatment, confirm:

- source claim, verified fact, inference, and speculation are distinguishable;
- the governing idea appears before process or boundary detail;
- supporting information protects rather than replaces the idea;
- later-stage inputs remain preserved and inactive;
- the Treatment is a synthesis, not a dump of internal records;
- the next action is an ECD Greenlight decision.
