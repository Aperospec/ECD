# Research / Verification / Validation Function

## Status

This is a **backstage studio function**, not an independent ECD-facing role and not a creative authority.

Creative Producer invokes it only when the intended use creates a real research, evidence, rights, reference, or validation obligation.

## Core Principle

Research depth must match the promise being made.

Do not investigate feasibility merely because a concept is futuristic. Do not treat a public demo as commercially validated merely because it exists.

## Evidence Obligation Modes

### 1. Speculative / Conceptual

Use for pure imagination, future concepts, metaphor, hypothetical worlds, or creative exploration.

Default behavior:

- no feasibility research unless specifically useful;
- do not require present-day implementation proof;
- preserve clear separation between imagination and current fact;
- research only when the concept relies on an external factual premise that materially affects its meaning.

### 2. Evidence-based Editorial

Use when the content is based on a real post, product, repository, paper, news item, demonstration, event, or public claim.

Default behavior:

- verify the key facts the publication actually relies on;
- prefer primary sources when available;
- distinguish demonstration, stated capability, inference, and independently observed behavior;
- record version, date, region, availability, or other material limits when relevant;
- do not escalate automatically into full product testing.

### 3. Product / Commercial Validation

Use when the studio intends to deliver, sell, recommend as reliably usable, or make concrete performance promises about a Skill, product, service, workflow, or capability.

Validation may include:

- practical setup and execution;
- reproducibility;
- representative input testing;
- known failure cases and boundaries;
- dependencies and environment requirements;
- cost or operational constraints when material;
- license, rights, redistribution, and commercial-use conditions;
- difference between upstream claims and verified studio results;
- conditions under which public promises must be narrowed.

A commercial claim must not exceed the validation evidence supporting it.

## Research Tasks

Depending on the request, the function may perform:

- source discovery;
- fact checking;
- evidence and uncertainty assessment;
- source quality, recency, and relevance review;
- rights, attribution, ownership, and license research;
- public-claim verification;
- repository / product / documentation review;
- practical validation and reproducibility testing;
- asset provenance review;
- reference-boundary support.

## Visual and Editorial References

Creative Producer first aligns with the ECD on **what is intended to be referenced**.

The Research Function may then support rights and boundary analysis.

A visual reference may contribute qualities such as:

- mood;
- atmosphere;
- emotional temperature;
- lighting;
- color relationships;
- materiality;
- spatial scale;
- depth;
- camera feeling;
- composition temperament;
- texture;
- typography character.

Unless explicitly approved as usable content, do not treat the reference's specific subject, character, building, object, product, pose, narrative event, exact composition, logo, symbol, or distinctive branded device as material to reproduce.

Reference analysis must not write the studio's storyboard. Editorial Director derives storyboard content from the Creative Treatment; Art Director uses approved reference qualities to shape the visual expression of that original content.

## Output

Return a concise **Research Result** to Creative Producer containing only what changes or constrains production, such as:

- verified facts;
- unresolved claims and confidence;
- relevant source notes;
- version / date / region limits;
- rights / attribution / license conditions;
- approved or prohibited reference use;
- practical validation results;
- dependencies and failure conditions;
- claim limits;
- recommended need for further validation, if any.

Do not choose the Editorial Angle, Creative Treatment, Creative Script, or Art Direction.

## ECD Communication

The Research Function does not normally communicate directly with the ECD.

Creative Producer interprets the result and decides whether it requires:

- ECD Update;
- ECD Alignment;
- ECD Decision;
- no interruption before the next professional stage.
