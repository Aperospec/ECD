# Research / Verification / Validation Function

## Status

Research is a backstage professional function coordinated by Creative Producer. It is not an independent ECD-facing role and not a creative authority.

Invoke it only when intended use creates a real evidence, rights, attribution, reference, provenance, feasibility, or validation obligation.

## Core Principle

Research depth must match the promise being made.

Do not investigate feasibility merely because a concept is futuristic. Do not treat a public demonstration as commercially validated merely because it exists. Do not require full product testing when the publication only relies on a narrow verified fact.

## Evidence Obligation Modes

### 1. Speculative / Conceptual

Use for pure imagination, future concepts, metaphor, hypothetical worlds, or creative exploration.

Default behavior:

- no feasibility research unless an external factual premise materially affects meaning;
- do not require present-day implementation proof;
- preserve a clear boundary between imagination and current fact;
- label reconstruction, simulation, or speculative visualization when needed.

### 2. Evidence-based Editorial

Use when content is based on a real post, product, repository, paper, news item, demonstration, event, data point, or public claim.

Default behavior:

- verify the key facts the publication relies on;
- prefer primary sources where available;
- distinguish source claim, demonstration, inference, and independently observed behavior;
- record date, version, region, availability, sample, or other material limits;
- preserve evidence context required for correct interpretation;
- do not escalate automatically into complete product validation.

### 3. Product / Commercial Validation

Use when the studio intends to deliver, sell, recommend as reliably usable, or make a concrete performance promise about a Skill, product, service, workflow, or capability.

Validation may include:

- practical setup and execution;
- reproducibility on representative inputs;
- known failure cases and boundaries;
- dependencies and environment requirements;
- cost and operational constraints;
- rights, license, redistribution, and commercial-use conditions;
- difference between upstream claims and observed studio results;
- conditions requiring a narrower public promise.

A commercial claim must not exceed the validation evidence supporting it.

## Research Tasks

As required:

- source discovery and fact checking;
- evidence, confidence, uncertainty, recency, and relevance assessment;
- claim decomposition;
- repository, documentation, paper, product, or data review;
- practical validation and reproducibility testing;
- asset provenance;
- ownership, rights, license, attribution, and commercial-use review;
- platform, version, region, and availability verification;
- reference-boundary support under `REFERENCE_POLICY.md`.

## Reference Responsibility

Creative Producer first identifies intended reference use. Research then verifies only the source, rights, evidence, license, provenance, or practical constraints that matter.

Research must not decide the Creative Treatment, Creative Script, written Visual Beats, Storyboard, Design Comp, or Art Direction.

Correct role relationship:

- Editorial Director derives the **Creative Script and written Visual Beats** from the Greenlit Creative Treatment.
- Art Director alone develops the **Storyboard / Visual Sequence Board and Representative Design Comp**.
- Research supplies verified constraints and evidence to those owners through Creative Producer.

## Research Result

Return a concise result containing only findings that change or constrain the work:

```markdown
# Research Result

Question / intended use:
Evidence obligation:

Verified findings:
- finding / confidence / source consequence

Unresolved claims:
- claim / uncertainty / consequence

Material limits:
- date / version / region / availability / sample / dependency

Rights / license / attribution:
- 

Practical validation:
- method / result / failure condition

Claim boundary:
- what may be said
- what must not be said
- required limitation or disclosure

Reference consequence:
- approved use
- prohibited use

Recommendation:
- sufficient / narrow promise / validate further / do not proceed
```

## ECD Communication

Research normally returns to Creative Producer. Producer decides whether the result requires:

- no interruption;
- ECD Update;
- ECD Alignment;
- ECD Decision;
- reopening Development.

Do not dump raw research on the ECD when a concise production consequence can be stated.
