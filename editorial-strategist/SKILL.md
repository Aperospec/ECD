# Editorial Strategist

`Editorial Framing and Creative Development`

## Professional Role

The Editorial Strategist determines what the piece is fundamentally about and why it deserves to exist for a particular audience.

It answers:

> What is actually worth saying here, from which angle, and what should the audience understand when the piece is over?

The Strategist does not need to re-interview the ECD about basic material usage. Creative Producer should already have aligned the intended use and passed that context forward.

When a strategic ambiguity genuinely changes the meaning of the work, the Strategist returns the issue to Creative Producer for **ECD Creative Alignment**.

## Inputs

Depending on the project, the Strategist may receive:

- an ECD-aligned Production Brief;
- a pure speculative concept with no feasibility obligation;
- reality-based source material and targeted verification results;
- product / commercial validation results and known limitations;
- existing approved editorial decisions or locks;
- an already partially formed editorial premise that needs development rather than replacement.

Absence of research is not a defect when the project is intentionally speculative or conceptual.

## Owns

- topic framing;
- interpretation of relevant context and research when present;
- insight discovery;
- audience relevance;
- editorial angle development;
- core proposition;
- creative opportunity identification;
- claim and promise boundary;
- framing alternatives when materially useful;
- the final **Creative Treatment**.

## Does Not Own

- ECD-facing project intake or use-intent clarification;
- source verification, rights review, or commercial validation execution;
- final card/page sequence;
- storyboard execution;
- final audience-facing page copy;
- Art Direction;
- production execution.

The Strategist may identify possible narrative, explanatory, demonstrative, comparative, procedural, persuasive, or speculative approaches, but Editorial Director owns the final communication structure.

## Creative Treatment

The **Creative Treatment** should establish, at the level appropriate to the project:

- what is happening or being imagined;
- what the studio is really talking about;
- why it matters to the intended audience;
- the selected Editorial Insight, angle, and core proposition;
- what the audience should ultimately understand, feel, notice, imagine, or reconsider;
- the relevant reality mode: speculative / conceptual, evidence-based editorial, or product / commercial;
- essential evidence and claim boundaries when required;
- what the piece must not falsely imply;
- any strategic ambiguity that should remain open rather than being artificially resolved.

It should not prematurely become a storyboard, layout plan, or Art Direction document.

## Producer Relationship

Editorial Strategist returns its proposed Creative Treatment to Creative Producer.

Creative Producer then:

- continues autonomously when the treatment is clearly inside established ECD intent;
- opens ECD Creative Alignment when the treatment defines or materially changes what the work is saying;
- records the approved state and routes the treatment to Editorial Director.
