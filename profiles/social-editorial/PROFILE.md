# Social Editorial Profile — V3

## Purpose

This profile configures ECD V3 for mobile-first social editorial work: covers, carousels, visual essays, explainers, product stories, evidence-led posts, campaign key visuals, and platform-specific image sequences.

The profile defines required department capabilities and deliverables. It does not replace department professional Skills.

## Default department path

```text
Creative Development Department
→ Editorial Department
→ Visual Department
→ Production Department
```

Creative Producer may compress internal records or combine execution seats for Compact work, but Department Review, Producer integration, ECD authority, and professional responsibility remain distinct.

## Development requirements

Default specialist capabilities:

- Creative Strategy;
- Concept Development;
- audience and platform reading;
- proportionate research / claims / rights review.

Required output:

- One-Sentence Creative Core;
- Creative Treatment;
- accepted development decisions and boundaries;
- Department Review Record;
- Director recommendation;
- Producer-integrated ECD Greenlight object.

## Editorial requirements

Default specialist capabilities:

- Content Architecture;
- Frame Script Writing;
- Copywriting;
- Copy Editing;
- Proofreading.

Conditional capabilities:

- Fact / Evidence Editing;
- Localization / Transcreation;
- Naming and terminology control.

Required output:

- Overall Communication Logic;
- complete sequence;
- one editorial job per page;
- concrete Frame Script for every page;
- exact page copy;
- complete companion / publication copy;
- evidence, source, and limitation placement;
- Department Review Record;
- Producer-integrated ECD Script Alignment object.

## Visual requirements

Mandatory specialist capabilities for every text-bearing project:

- Editorial Design;
- Typography;
- Design Critique.

Additional default capability for multi-page work:

- Storyboard / Sequence Design.

Conditional capabilities:

- Visual Concept when the governing visual idea is unresolved;
- Image Direction when imagery must be created or materially transformed;
- Information Design for processes, diagrams, data, evidence, comparison, UI, or annotation;
- Motion / Interaction Direction for animated or interactive surfaces.

Required visual evidence:

### Full-sequence coverage

Show every page or state at sufficient fidelity to judge:

- dominant visual gesture;
- content and copy mass;
- page differentiation;
- sequence rhythm;
- shared visual grammar;
- cover-to-payoff progression.

### Representative fidelity proof

Show the smallest sufficient set of high-fidelity page classes using exact copy and credible imagery to prove:

- composition and hierarchy;
- typography and line behavior;
- image–type relationship;
- evidence treatment;
- mobile readability;
- production feasibility.

### Professional review

Art Director must inspect actual design evidence and include an independent Design Critique report before Department clearance.

Required output:

- visual problem and selected visual thesis;
- concept-route evidence when required;
- full-sequence contact sheet or equivalent;
- representative design comps;
- typography specification and proofs;
- image direction and proofs when required;
- information-design proof when required;
- target-width and platform proof;
- Department Review Record;
- Producer-integrated ECD Visual Alignment object when governing decisions require authority.

## Production requirements

Default specialist capabilities:

- Finished Art;
- Production Typesetting;
- Variant Production;
- Technical QA.

Conditional capabilities:

- Image Production;
- Retouch / Compositing;
- asset sourcing and provenance management;
- output engineering for unusual formats.

Required output:

- final assets at target geometry;
- exact text verification;
- visual fidelity proof;
- image and compositing QA;
- all required platform / language / size variants;
- mobile, thumbnail, safe-area, and UI-overlay proof;
- file, format, color, naming, and opening checks;
- Department Review Record;
- Editorial, Visual, and Production Director sign-offs;
- Producer-integrated ECD Final Acceptance object.

## Social surface logic

The profile may select among communication modes such as:

- Poster — one idea, one dominant impact;
- Editorial Cover — one visual center with multiple meaningful entry points;
- Hybrid — poster force with editorial depth;
- Social Editorial Sequence — one screen, one primary message; one sequence, one communication progression;
- Evidence-led Sequence — proof or source material carries the visual authority;
- Process / Systems Sequence — relationships, steps, roles, or transformation must remain legible.

Mode selection belongs to the relevant department Skills. The user is not required to choose a mode unless they wish to override the recommendation.

## Platform conditions

Creative Producer records:

- platform and placement;
- aspect ratio and target dimensions;
- feed thumbnail behavior;
- swipe or scroll behavior;
- caption availability;
- safe areas and UI overlays;
- expected viewing width and duration;
- language and localization variants;
- publication and rights constraints.

When the platform is unknown, use a conservative mobile-first condition rather than desktop assumptions.

## Visual quality standard

A Social Editorial design should demonstrate:

- a specific visual idea tied to the content;
- strong but meaningful hierarchy;
- deliberate composition and crop;
- typography treated as content and form;
- image and type operating as one system;
- purposeful negative space;
- page-to-page rhythm and variation;
- evidence that remains inspectable;
- actual mobile readability;
- finish appropriate to publication.

Numeric ratios and scores may be used as diagnostics, never as substitutes for professional judgment.

## Common failures

Reject or rework:

- generic title-top / image-middle / caption-bottom layouts;
- timid titles placed only in a safe corner;
- all pages using the same template;
- decorative microtype that cannot be read on a phone;
- opaque cards used because composition was not solved;
- image and type behaving as unrelated layers;
- a beautiful image with no credible place for exact copy;
- flat cutout treatment that destroys volume or material presence;
- evidence too small to inspect;
- pages split from one paragraph without distinct editorial jobs;
- final production that differs materially from Department Cleared design.

## Compact projects

Compact work may merge specialist execution seats and combine proof artifacts, but must still provide:

- Department Director review;
- exact content and copy;
- at least one genuine design exploration before final choice;
- target-size proof;
- independent visual critique;
- Producer integrated review;
- required ECD authority.

## Authority gates

- Creative Treatment → ECD Greenlight;
- every new Creative Script → ECD Script Alignment;
- new or materially changed governing visual system → ECD Visual Alignment;
- final deliverables → ECD Final Acceptance.

Creative Producer controls release and stage activation. Department Directors control professional quality within their departments.
