# Concept Reframing — Social Editorial Profile V3.2

Constitution clause: `SEM-049`.

## Purpose

Concept Reframing turns a publicly visible topic, idea, demonstration, discussion, or high-engagement social post into an independently written and independently visualized Social Editorial work.

The discovery source helps the studio find a topic worth discussing. It does not automatically become the subject, structure, wording, imagery, attribution line, or public evidence of the new work.

This mode is appropriate when the ECD wants to:

- identify a topic that has already attracted audience interest;
- explain the underlying idea in the studio's own way;
- use original examples, scenarios, copy, and visual material;
- publish a concept-sharing post rather than a tutorial, review, or reproduction report.

## Source posture

Classify the source relationship before Development.

### 1. Discovery Signal

The source is used to discover or rank a topic.

It may contribute:

- evidence of audience interest;
- a broad subject area;
- a question, tension, or concept worth independently developing.

It does not authorize or require transfer of:

- the original wording;
- the original sequence;
- the original examples;
- the original visual concept or composition;
- screenshots, images, video, or other source assets;
- the creator's identity as part of the final publication.

When the final work has no material factual dependency on the source and uses no direct source material, public attribution is not required by this profile by default.

### 2. Factual Dependency

The new work relies on a specific current fact, product behavior, quoted statement, event, result, or demonstration.

Verify only the factual units the final publication actually depends on. Do not convert a narrow editorial dependency into full product validation.

Public source identification or attribution is included only when:

- it is necessary for an accurate claim;
- quotation, direct evidence, platform rules, or rights conditions require it;
- the ECD explicitly requests it.

### 3. Direct-use Material

The final work directly uses or recognizably transforms source wording, screenshots, images, video, audio, data, interface captures, or other protected expression.

Apply the Reference Contract and Claims / Rights review. Required permission, attribution, or replacement cannot be waived by silently treating direct use as topic discovery.

## Independent editorial development

A Concept Reframing project must establish its own:

- editorial angle;
- governing proposition;
- communication progression;
- examples or scenarios;
- wording and rhetorical structure;
- visual concept;
- imagery, layout, and typography.

The final work should not be a sentence-level paraphrase, structural copy, or visual remake of the discovery source.

The internal originality test asks:

- Does the work still stand if the discovery source is removed?
- Does it use a distinct angle, structure, example set, copy, and visual system?
- Is any source-specific expression present only when separately authorized?

This test remains backstage unless it creates a real publication issue.

## Evidence and validation behavior

Engagement metrics are a topic-selection signal. They are not proof of factual accuracy, reliability, or repeatability.

At the same time, a Concept Reframing post does not require independent reproduction merely because the topic originated in a public demonstration.

Select Evidence Obligation from the claims actually made by the new publication:

- a conceptual discussion using invented or illustrative examples may remain `Speculative / Conceptual`;
- a post that states a real tool, product, event, or demonstration fact uses `Evidence-based Editorial` for those specific claims;
- `Product / Commercial Validation` activates only when the current project sells, recommends, or promises reliable operation, repeatability, compatibility, cost, or deployment value.

A future idea to productize the concept is recorded as a Deferred project seed. It does not raise the current post to Commercial Validation when the current publication contains no offer or reliability promise.

## Public-language defaults

For a Discovery Signal project, do not automatically insert:

- the original creator's name or handle;
- `灵感来源于……`;
- `我们实测了……`;
- `我们没有实测……`;
- `这只是概念创作`;
- `画面并非真实运行过程`;
- defensive legal or validation disclaimers;
- a sales or future-productization preview.

These statements appear only when the actual publication claim, direct-use condition, audience interpretation, or explicit ECD instruction makes them necessary.

The absence of an unnecessary disclaimer does not authorize a false claim. The publication simply states its own idea in its own voice without discussing tests, sources, or provenance that are not part of the editorial proposition.

## Original visuals

The studio may create its own photographs, illustrations, generated scenes, examples, diagrams, or poster designs to express the independently developed concept.

Original studio-created visuals are part of the new editorial work. They need no source-facing explanation merely because the topic was discovered elsewhere.

Visual Development must still avoid copying source-specific composition, characters, branded expression, or recognizably protected assets unless separately authorized.

## Quality gate

Pass when:

- engagement is used to select a topic, not to prove unrelated claims;
- the new work has its own angle, structure, examples, copy, and visuals;
- Discovery Signal does not automatically trigger public attribution or defensive explanation;
- factual dependencies receive proportionate verification;
- direct-use material receives the required rights treatment;
- no reproduction or reliability claim is invented;
- future productization remains outside the current post unless explicitly commissioned.
