# Writing Reference Canon

## Purpose

This canon supplies method sources for the internal Copy Desk.

Books and standards are treated as compressed craft systems. Extract generalized principles; write original copy; do not reproduce source text, signature examples, or a named author's recognizable style.

## Priority order

When methods conflict, prioritize:

1. fidelity to the Greenlit Treatment;
2. semantic and grammatical correctness;
3. idiomatic language for the target locale;
4. reader comprehension under the intended viewing condition;
5. voice, rhythm, memorability, and rhetorical effect.

## Chinese core canon

### 叶圣陶《怎样写作》

Operational contribution:

- write from a real intended meaning;
- orient prose toward communication and the reader;
- prefer intelligibility before ornament;
- treat revision as part of composition;
- use natural modern Chinese appropriate to the context.

### 吕叔湘、朱德熙《语法修辞讲话》

Operational contribution:

- diagnose vocabulary, function words, sentence structure, expression, and punctuation;
- test collocation, word order, reference, conjunction, and clause relation;
- distinguish grammatical possibility from effective expression;
- correct the earliest linguistic defect.

### 王鼎钧《作文七巧》

Operational contribution:

- select narration, description, reasoning, induction, deduction, or synthesis according to the material;
- develop ordinary material through selection, order, contrast, and detail;
- distinguish progression from a topic list.

### 陈望道《修辞学发凡》

Operational contribution:

- make rhetoric serve purpose, context, audience, and emotional temperature;
- distinguish expressive choice from decoration;
- use metaphor, contrast, repetition, and parallelism only when their relation to meaning is clear.

### GB/T 15834—2011《标点符号用法》

Operational contribution:

- provide the baseline for Simplified Chinese punctuation;
- align punctuation with syntax, hierarchy, quotation, range, and tone.

## English core canon

### William Zinsser — *On Writing Well*

Operational contribution:

- clarity, simplicity, human voice, and revision;
- remove clutter while preserving nuance;
- replace institutional prose with reader-oriented nonfiction.

### Steven Pinker — *The Sense of Style*

Operational contribution:

- reveal agents, actions, and causal relationships;
- write from the reader's knowledge state;
- build coherence across sentences;
- control abstraction and the curse of knowledge.

### Verlyn Klinkenborg — *Several Short Sentences About Writing*

Operational contribution:

- treat each sentence as a deliberate unit;
- judge what the sentence actually says;
- develop cadence and pressure through sequence;
- inspect fragments and continuation closely.

### Roy Peter Clark — *Writing Tools*

Operational contribution:

- use strong verbs and purposeful order;
- control emphasis through beginnings, endings, detail, punctuation, and sentence length;
- connect sentence craft to larger structure.

### Benjamin Dreyer — *Dreyer's English*

Operational contribution:

- copyediting discipline for grammar, usage, punctuation, consistency, capitalization, compounds, and word choice;
- distinguish real errors from arbitrary prohibitions;
- apply coherent house decisions.

## Advertising and social copy canon

### Luke Sullivan — *Hey, Whipple, Squeeze This*

Operational contribution:

- begin with the idea and audience tension;
- make copy and image divide the work;
- ensure every line earns attention;
- prefer a precise concept to generic promotional language.

### D&AD — *The Copy Book*

Operational contribution:

- study copy as a relationship among idea, audience, medium, and execution;
- use strong work as a benchmark for decision quality rather than a template.

## Canon-to-workflow map

| Craft question | Primary method layer |
|---|---|
| Is the intended meaning clear? | Ye Shengtao, Zinsser, Pinker |
| Is the sentence structurally and idiomatically sound? | Lü / Zhu, Dreyer |
| Does the material progress rather than accumulate? | Wang Dingjun, Clark |
| Is rhetoric serving meaning? | Chen Wangdao, Klinkenborg |
| Do copy and image divide the work effectively? | Sullivan, D&AD |
| Are mechanics consistent? | GB/T 15834, Dreyer |

## Use standard

The canon is successful only when it changes a Copy Brief, candidate set, edit, read-aloud result, or quality decision. Source names remain background references; execution uses the operational protocols in this directory.
