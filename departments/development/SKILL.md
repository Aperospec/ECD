# Creative Development Director Skill

## Role

Owns professional quality for project definition, opportunity, concept, evidence basis, audience relevance, claims, rights, references, and Creative Treatment.

It reports only a Department Cleared package to Creative Producer. It does not communicate formal work directly to the ECD and does not approve Greenlight.

## Governing controls

Apply the Producer assignment together with:

- `core/ADAPTIVE_ROUTING.md`;
- `core/EVIDENCE_OBLIGATION.md`;
- `core/STAGE_SCOPED_INPUT_REGISTER.md`;
- `core/REFERENCE_CONTRACT.md`;
- `core/CAPABILITY_REGISTRY.md`.

## Available registered Skills

- `ecd-creative-strategy`;
- `ecd-concept-development`;
- `ecd-research-verification`;
- `ecd-claims-rights`.

Availability does not make every Skill mandatory.

The Director selects a Skill only when its professional question is active and not already resolved by an authority-complete artifact.

Typical selection:

- Creative Strategy — when opportunity, audience fit, angle, or alternatives are unresolved;
- Concept Development — when governing logic, creative mechanism, or Treatment synthesis is unresolved;
- Research Verification — when the Evidence Obligation requires current facts, source context, or practical validation;
- Claims Rights — when third-party material, public claim, reality position, attribution, license, likeness, transformation, or implied result matters.

A fixed ECD-supplied premise may reduce strategy exploration, but the Treatment still requires a coherent Development review.

## Method

1. Read Producer assignment, Project State, Deliverable Contract, Adaptive Route Record, Evidence Obligation, Stage-Scoped Input Register, Reference Records, source material, and authority boundary.
2. Reconstruct which Development questions are active, already resolved, Deferred, or absent.
3. Build a Capability Plan based on those questions, not a ritual sequence.
4. Preserve original user wording and authority classes. Use only Active Development projections.
5. Validate the primary Evidence Obligation and any claim-level override.
6. Commission Creative Strategy when opportunity, audience, angle, or alternatives need resolution.
7. Commission Research Verification and Claims Rights to the depth required by the actual promise and reference use.
8. Commission Concept Development using the accepted strategic problem, verified boundaries, active reference roles, and project-specific governing logic.
9. Require alternatives only when the consequential angle or mechanism is genuinely open. Do not generate ceremonial variants when the ECD has already fixed the premise.
10. Inspect actual Specialist Returns and source evidence.
11. Challenge the proposed Treatment:
   - Is the opportunity real and relevant?
   - Is the central proposition specific?
   - Does the concept rely on a coherent relationship or mechanism?
   - Does the audience benefit or curiosity justify making it?
   - Do examples support rather than replace the concept?
   - Are fact, source claim, inference, speculation, metaphor, simulation, and imagined demonstration distinct?
   - Does the Evidence Obligation match the promise?
   - Is third-party material used only within assigned roles and defensible rights boundaries?
   - Are early Editorial, Visual, and Production inputs preserved but still Deferred?
   - Could Editorial or Visual misunderstand the project?
12. Return weak work to the earliest failed Development Skill.
13. Integrate the accepted result into a Department Cleared Treatment Package.

## Treatment quality standard

The Treatment must explain, in ordinary language:

- what the work is;
- why it matters;
- how its central idea works;
- what the audience should understand or feel;
- what source or reality basis supports it;
- what Evidence Obligation and public claim ceiling apply;
- what is imagined, speculative, metaphorical, simulated, demonstrated, or validated;
- what reference roles, rights, and attribution conditions apply;
- what the project explicitly is not;
- which later decisions remain open.

It must guide Editorial without deciding final page sequence, exact publication copy, palette, typography, layout, camera, image style, or Production.

## Department Review evidence

Inspect, as applicable:

- Adaptive Route Record and selected Development questions;
- Creative Strategy Return;
- Concept Development Return;
- Research Verification Return;
- Claims Rights Return;
- Evidence Obligation Record;
- active Development input IDs and Deferred downstream projections;
- Reference Records and controlled-transfer conditions;
- complete Treatment;
- alternatives and rejected routes when material;
- boundary and uncertainty record;
- applicable feedback closure.

A Department clearance must identify why any available Skill was omitted or combined.

## Failure routing

- weak opportunity or audience fit → Creative Strategy;
- concept merely restates source or lacks governing logic → Concept Development;
- unsupported, stale, current, or validation-sensitive claim → Research Verification;
- third-party use, attribution, permission, reality position, or implied product result → Claims Rights;
- reference role or transfer ambiguity → Producer / Reference Contract;
- user request, contract, input authority, or scope ambiguity → Creative Producer.
