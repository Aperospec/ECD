# Research Verification Skill

## Purpose

Research Verification establishes what can responsibly support the project.

It does not choose the final creative angle, write promotional claims, or use a source merely because it is vivid.

## Inputs

- research question and intended downstream use;
- source links, posts, files, screenshots, or datasets;
- current strategic or concept options;
- date sensitivity;
- evidence, quotation, and attribution requirements;
- Development Director assignment.

## Method

### 1. Define the decision the research serves

Record:

- what must be known;
- why it affects the project;
- what level of certainty is required;
- what would change if the answer differs.

### 2. Inspect primary material

Prefer the original post, product documentation, research paper, dataset, official statement, or creator explanation when available.

Record source identity, date, author, context, and access limitations.

### 3. Separate evidence classes

For every material statement classify:

- verified fact;
- source's own claim;
- reasonable inference;
- interpretation;
- speculation;
- unknown or disputed;
- temporally unstable fact requiring current verification.

### 4. Check context and contradiction

Inspect whether:

- a quotation is truncated;
- a demonstration is mistaken for a product capability;
- a concept example is mistaken for an actual result;
- a source date makes the claim stale;
- another reliable source materially disagrees;
- a screenshot omits qualifying context.

### 5. Create claim-ready language

Provide the strongest accurate wording and, where useful, a safer alternative.

Do not overstate certainty for dramatic effect.

### 6. Record unresolved limits

State what could not be verified and how that limits the Treatment or public copy.

## Self-check

- Did the work inspect the relevant original source where possible?
- Are facts separate from inference and source claims?
- Are current facts actually current?
- Is the proposed wording no stronger than the evidence?
- Did research inform rather than replace creative judgment?

## Failure routing

- permission, copyright, quotation, or transformation issue → Claims Rights;
- research undermines the proposed opportunity → Creative Strategy;
- research changes the concept's premise → Concept Development;
- source cannot be accessed or verified → Development Director / Producer blocker.
