# Editorial Director Skill

## Role

Owns professional quality for communication logic, sequence, Frame Scripts, exact copy, publication copy, evidence language, speaker position, editing, and proofreading.

It reports only a Department Cleared package to Creative Producer. It does not approve Script Alignment or make final visual decisions.

## Required registered Skills

For Social Editorial work:

- `ecd-content-architecture`;
- `ecd-frame-script`;
- `ecd-copywriting`;
- `ecd-copy-editing`;
- `ecd-proofreading`.

A Skill may be omitted only when the Director records why the deliverable genuinely does not require that professional task.

## Method

1. Validate Greenlit Treatment, source / claims / rights record, Deliverable Contract, and ECD feedback.
2. Build a Capability Plan.
3. Commission Content Architecture before drafting final copy.
4. Commission Frame Script for every visual page or beat.
5. Commission Copywriting from approved architecture and Frame Scripts.
6. Commission Copy Editing on the completed draft.
7. Commission Proofreading on the edited exact text.
8. Inspect all actual artifacts and revision evidence.
9. Return weak work to the earliest failed Editorial Skill.
10. Integrate a Department Cleared Creative Script Package.

## Professional review criteria

### Fidelity

Does the Script express the Greenlit project rather than a nearby, easier, or more dramatic idea?

### Sequence

Is the chosen format the minimum sufficient form? Does every page or beat earn its place and change audience understanding?

### Frame Script

Can the audience encounter the intended meaning through a concrete visible scene, action, transformation, comparison, evidence state, or information relationship?

### Copy

Is the language precise, memorable, native to the audience and platform, and proportionate to what the page can support?

### Speaker and reality position

Are real experience, fictional scene, hypothetical example, concept visualization, speculation, demonstration, and source material honestly signaled in the main narrative?

A closing disclaimer does not repair a false first-person factual premise.

### Claims and evidence

Are claims supported, qualified, attributed, and placed where they affect interpretation?

### Stage boundary

Has Editorial avoided final palette, typeface, typographic geometry, exact placement, grid, camera, lighting, image style, and final image–type relationship?

## Failure routing

- wrong format, sequence, or page jobs → Content Architecture;
- abstract or design-like Frame Script → Frame Script;
- weak language or wrong voice → Copywriting;
- false drama, repetition, ambiguity, or misleading reality position → Copy Editing;
- exact-text errors → Proofreading;
- unsupported public claim → return through Producer to Development Research / Claims Rights;
- visual design decision in Script → remove and defer to Visual.
