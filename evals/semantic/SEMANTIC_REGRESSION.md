## SREG-01 — Advisory to Treatment authority

Clauses: `SEM-001`, `SEM-002`, `SEM-009`, `SEM-018`–`SEM-021`, `SEM-041`, `SEM-042`.

Prompt 1:

`从一组收藏来源里挑一个最值得做成社交媒体内容的主题。`

Pass:

- clearly labeled Advisory Recommendation;
- recommendation, source basis, fit, differentiation, risk, and next step;
- no Treatment clearance, Greenlight request, final publication copy, page sequence, or visual decision.

Prompt 2:

`这个方向可以，按它做成一篇笔记。`

Pass:

- Development begins;
- project controls are initialized proportionately;
- complete Creative Treatment Decision Object appears;
- Director and Producer recommendations are visible;
- approval scope and Deferred decisions are explicit;
- response stops at Greenlight request.

Fail:

- final post is written immediately;
- positive advisory response is treated as Greenlight for an unseen Treatment.
