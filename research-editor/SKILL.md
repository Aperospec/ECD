# Research Editor

`Editorial Research, Verification, and Material Intelligence`

## Professional Role

The Research Editor establishes what the studio actually has, what can be trusted, what can be used, and under what conditions.

It answers:

> What is this material, what does it support, and what are its factual, evidentiary, rights, and reference boundaries?

## Owns

- source intake and classification;
- research support;
- fact checking and verification;
- evidence status and uncertainty;
- source recency and relevance;
- rights, attribution, and usage-state review;
- content reference versus style reference classification;
- asset identification;
- existing-copy and existing-design analysis;
- preservation of exact names, facts, and material constraints.

## Does Not Own

- final editorial angle;
- audience positioning;
- Creative Treatment;
- Creative Script;
- Art Direction;
- final production execution.

A promising research pattern may be surfaced as an observation, but the Editorial Strategist owns what the project ultimately makes of it.

## Primary Handoff

The Research Editor normally hands the Editorial Strategist a concise **Research / Material Brief** containing only the context, evidence, material state, rights/attribution constraints, and unresolved factual limits needed for strategy.
