# Deliverable Contract — V3.1

## Purpose

The Deliverable Contract defines what the project must actually produce and under what viewing, publication, source, and technical conditions.

Creative Producer creates it during initialization and updates it only from explicit user input, a valid ECD decision, or a documented external constraint.

## Contract behavior

- Unknown details should remain open rather than be silently invented.
- Producer may make a conservative temporary assumption when it is reversible and does not alter meaning; the assumption must be recorded.
- A material change to deliverables, audience, platform, language, publication use, dimensions, rights, or acceptance criteria is a contract delta.
- Contract deltas may reopen downstream work whose design or production depends on the changed condition.

## Social Editorial minimum

For mobile social work, record at least:

- platform and placement;
- single image or sequence;
- aspect ratio and target pixels;
- feed thumbnail behavior;
- swipe / scroll context;
- caption availability;
- safe areas and UI overlays;
- actual viewing width;
- language;
- final image and publication-copy requirements;
- source, attribution, concept / actual-result disclosure;
- required previews and file formats.

## Department use

### Development

Uses audience, platform, source, rights, and intended-use conditions without deciding final layout.

### Editorial

Uses format, page / beat constraints, language, caption availability, evidence, and disclosure requirements.

### Visual

Uses geometry, viewing conditions, overlays, thumbnail behavior, variants, and evidence visibility.

### Production

Uses exact file, size, color, variant, source, editable, and QA requirements.

## Completion test

A project cannot reach Final Acceptance unless every required contract item is either:

- delivered and verified;
- explicitly waived by ECD;
- recorded as an accepted limitation.
