# Deliverable Contract — V3.1

## Purpose

The Deliverable Contract defines what the project must actually produce and under what viewing, publication, evidence, source, rights, language, and technical conditions.

Creative Producer creates it during initialization and updates it only from explicit user input, a valid ECD decision, a documented external constraint, or an authorized contract delta.

The contract is paired with:

- `ADAPTIVE_ROUTING.md` for process depth;
- `EVIDENCE_OBLIGATION.md` for proof depth;
- `STAGE_SCOPED_INPUT_REGISTER.md` for early and Deferred inputs;
- `REFERENCE_CONTRACT.md` for source and reference use.

## Contract behavior

- Unknown details should remain open rather than be silently invented.
- Producer may make a conservative temporary assumption when it is reversible and does not alter meaning, rights, claim, scope, or irreversible action; the assumption must be recorded.
- A material change to deliverables, audience, platform, language, publication use, dimensions, evidence obligation, public claim, rights, reference use, or acceptance criteria is a contract delta.
- Contract deltas may reopen downstream work whose design or production depends on the changed condition.
- Information already recorded in the Stage-Scoped Input Register is carried forward; the ECD is not asked to repeat it merely because another department becomes active.

## Social Editorial minimum

For mobile social work, record at least:

- platform and placement;
- single image or sequence;
- aspect ratio and target pixels;
- feed thumbnail behavior;
- swipe / scroll context;
- caption availability;
- safe areas and UI overlays;
- actual viewing width;
- language and locale;
- final image and publication-copy requirements;
- Evidence Obligation and public claim ceiling;
- source, attribution, concept / actual-result disclosure;
- reference roles and direct-use conditions;
- required previews and file formats.

## Department use

### Development

Uses audience, platform, intended use, Evidence Obligation, source, rights, reference roles, and project boundaries without deciding final Editorial or Visual execution.

### Editorial

Uses format, page / beat constraints, language and locale, caption availability, evidence, disclosure, attribution, and language-variant requirements.

### Visual

Uses geometry, viewing conditions, overlays, thumbnail behavior, variants, evidence visibility, exact-copy consequences, and activated reference roles.

### Production

Uses exact file, size, color, variant, source, editable, attribution, evidence-preservation, and QA requirements.

## Completion test

A project cannot reach Final Acceptance unless every required contract item is either:

- delivered and verified;
- explicitly waived by ECD;
- recorded as an accepted limitation.

The final check must also confirm that:

- the Evidence Obligation was satisfied or narrowed honestly;
- the public claim does not exceed validation;
- reference uses match assigned roles and permissions;
- language variants received native or transcreation review when required;
- all stage-scoped inputs reached a resolved, superseded, rejected, or explicitly open state.
