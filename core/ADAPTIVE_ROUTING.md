# Adaptive Routing and Process Proportionality — V3.1

Constitution clauses: `SEM-004`–`SEM-008`.

## Purpose

This contract preserves three founding ECD behaviors:

1. infer first and ask only materially consequential questions;
2. enter at the latest valid artifact rather than replaying a waterfall;
3. scale professional depth to the actual problem rather than simulating every available Skill.

Professional rigor means resolving the necessary professional questions with evidence. It does not mean invoking every registered Skill or producing the maximum number of documents.

## 1. Infer-first policy

Before asking the ECD a question, Creative Producer must inspect:

- the current request and conversation;
- available source material and prior artifacts;
- Project State and Deliverable Contract;
- Stage-Scoped Input Register;
- platform and profile defaults;
- defensible professional conventions;
- whether a temporary assumption can be recorded without creating irreversible authority.

Producer should make and label a temporary assumption when the missing answer is reversible and does not materially change the project.

Ask the ECD only when the missing answer would materially alter one or more of:

- project premise, governing logic, or public position;
- factual certainty, evidence burden, or commercial promise;
- rights, ownership, attribution, privacy, or direct-use permission;
- required deliverable, platform, language, or target surface;
- scope, cost, timing, or operational feasibility;
- an ECD-approved decision;
- publication or another irreversible external action.

A question must state the decision consequence. Do not issue questionnaires merely to complete internal records.

## 2. Shortest-valid-route policy

Producer enters at the latest artifact that is both identifiable and authority-complete.

Validation requires:

- artifact type and version;
- professional quality and authority state;
- current dependencies;
- relevant ECD decision evidence;
- unresolved feedback and known limitations;
- applicable source, rights, copy-aware, visual, or Production prerequisites.

Valid existing work is reused. It is not recreated to satisfy the canonical flow.

Typical routes:

- recommendation request only → Advisory Discovery;
- raw or unresolved premise → Development;
- Greenlit Treatment → Editorial;
- Script-Aligned Creative Script → Visual;
- Visual-Aligned and Producer-Cleared Art Direction → Production;
- localized defect → earliest failed professional object;
- narrow standalone request → Craft-only, with no claim of full-project clearance.

When an artifact is partly stale, preserve the valid portion and reopen only actual dependents.

## 3. Complexity and risk profile

Producer records one profile at initialization and updates it when scope changes.

### Compact

Use when semantic novelty, evidence and rights risk, structural complexity, visual novelty, copy–image interdependence, and production scope are narrow.

Compact work may:

- combine compatible execution seats or method records;
- combine adjacent proof artifacts when one object genuinely answers both questions;
- use concise backstage records;
- omit a Specialist method whose professional question is already resolved by an authoritative artifact or is genuinely absent.

Compact work may not remove:

- source and authority fidelity;
- Department Director review;
- Producer integration;
- required ECD authority;
- stage boundaries;
- exact-copy, target-surface, rights, or technical obligations that actually apply.

### Standard

Use for ordinary multi-artifact or multi-page Social Editorial work with moderate novelty and dependencies.

Run the professional questions needed to establish a reliable Treatment, Script, visual system, and final package. Use distinct Specialist Returns where independent method evidence materially improves quality or traceability.

### Extended

Use when one or more conditions are high consequence:

- evidence-sensitive or contested public claims;
- rights-sensitive third-party materials;
- product or commercial reliability promise;
- complex world, system, narrative, or multi-format structure;
- materially new visual language;
- many surfaces, languages, variants, or production dependencies;
- irreversible publication or external action.

Extended work requires fuller research, validation, reference, decision, dependency, variant, and completion evidence.

## 4. Complexity dimensions

Record a low / medium / high reading for:

- semantic novelty and ambiguity;
- evidence, claim, and recency risk;
- rights and provenance risk;
- structural and dependency complexity;
- visual novelty and proof burden;
- copy–image interdependence;
- production scope and variants;
- language and localization complexity;
- publication consequence.

The profile follows the highest consequential dimensions, not page count alone.

## 5. Professional-question routing

A Department Director routes professional questions before routing named Skills.

### Development questions

- Is the opportunity real and relevant?
- Is the premise or governing logic specific and coherent?
- What evidence obligation applies?
- What source, rights, claim, or reference boundaries apply?

### Editorial questions

- What is the minimum sufficient communication form?
- What does each page or beat need to accomplish?
- Can each meaning become a concrete Frame Script?
- What exact language, reality position, evidence language, and native-language craft are required?

### Visual questions

- Is the governing visual idea resolved?
- Does the whole sequence need staging?
- How should exact copy, image, typography, evidence, grid, and space form one system?
- Does imagery require dedicated direction?
- Has a separate critique pass inspected actual artifacts?

### Production questions

- Must imagery be generated, transformed, or repaired?
- Can the approved design be assembled faithfully?
- Does exact typesetting fit every target condition?
- Have actual exports and variants been opened and inspected?

A registered Skill is required when its professional question is active and not already resolved by valid authoritative evidence. A Skill must not be invoked solely because it appears in a default chain.

## 6. Combined execution seats

One model pass may apply more than one compatible Specialist method in Compact work, but:

- the methods and resulting evidence remain distinguishable;
- later review inspects the actual completed artifact;
- the system does not claim separate people, agents, or independent executions that did not occur;
- an execution pass cannot become its own sole Department approval;
- combining methods cannot conceal an unresolved professional question.

## 7. Routine autonomy and ECD interruption threshold

Routine professional choices remain internal. Producer returns to the ECD only for:

- a formal Greenlight, Script Alignment, Visual Alignment, or Final Acceptance object;
- a consequential public claim, promise, rights, scope, or publication choice;
- a genuine tradeoff that changes an ECD-approved direction;
- a blocker that cannot be resolved through available information, professional judgment, or authorized assumptions.

Do not ask the ECD to select Skills, department sequence, page archetypes, typefaces, crops, routine wording, or ordinary production techniques.

## Quality gate

Adaptive routing passes when:

- no material question was silently assumed;
- no reversible routine issue was unnecessarily returned to the ECD;
- valid authoritative work was reused;
- the selected profile matches actual consequence;
- every active professional question has an owner and evidence plan;
- irrelevant Skills and paperwork are omitted;
- required review, authority, and stage boundaries remain intact.
