# Reference and Discovery-Source Contract — V3.2

Constitution clauses: `SEM-014`–`SEM-017`, `SEM-047`, `SEM-049`.

## Purpose

A source is not an undifferentiated template.

This contract preserves why material was supplied or discovered, which roles are active, what may transfer, what must remain source-specific, and how rights, evidence, interpretation, and public attribution survive across departments.

It applies to links, posts, screenshots, images, products, papers, repositories, interfaces, films, campaigns, styles, layouts, workflows, and other material sources.

## Discovery Signal versus material reference

A social post or other source may be used only as a **Discovery Signal**: it reveals a topic with audience interest but does not materially contribute facts, wording, assets, structure, or visual expression to the final work.

When classified as Discovery Signal:

- engagement may inform topic selection;
- the studio extracts only the broad public topic, question, tension, or concept;
- the new work develops its own angle, examples, communication progression, copy, and visuals;
- the source does not automatically become a public citation, attribution line, evidence asset, or visual reference;
- the original creator's identity remains backstage unless the ECD requests it or the final claim / direct-use condition requires it;
- no `灵感来源于`, `未实测`, `概念创作`, or similar source-facing language is added by default;
- source-specific wording, sequence, examples, composition, and protected expression remain excluded.

If the final work later depends on a specific source fact or uses source material directly, reclassify the affected use as `Source / Evidence` or `Authorized Content Asset` and apply the corresponding controls.

## Reference roles

Assign one or more explicit roles. Only assigned roles are active.

- **Discovery Signal** — topic discovery and audience-interest signal only.
- **Source / Evidence** — supports a factual statement or public claim.
- **Authorized Content Asset** — may be reproduced or transformed under recorded conditions.
- **Concept Reference** — contributes an abstract idea or governing relationship.
- **World / Structure Reference** — contributes spatial, systemic, narrative, or interaction organization.
- **Mood / Atmosphere Reference** — contributes emotional temperature, pace, or environmental feeling.
- **Style / Material Reference** — contributes formal or material qualities without authorizing copied expression.
- **Color / Light Reference** — contributes value, palette, illumination, or contrast principles.
- **Composition Reference** — contributes framing, mass, scale, depth, or reading-path principles.
- **Typography / Image–Type Reference** — contributes typographic behavior or image–type relationship.
- **Workflow / Interaction Reference** — contributes process, handoff, interface, or interaction logic.
- **Quality Benchmark** — defines a level of craft or finish, not a design to imitate.
- **Negative Reference** — identifies a direction, effect, or failure to avoid.

The visible prominence of an element in the source does not make that element part of the mandate.

## Four-pass reading

Material references are read through four distinct passes. A pure Discovery Signal may receive only enough inspection to identify the topic and exclude source-specific expression; do not perform unnecessary visual analysis when no visual role is active.

### 1. Description

Record observable content without interpreting meaning:

- subjects, objects, text, evidence, actions, interfaces;
- shape, scale, placement, depth, framing;
- light, value, color, texture, material;
- sequence, rhythm, movement, density;
- viewer position and relationship.

### 2. Formal analysis

Explain how observable parts are organized:

- dominant and secondary masses;
- figure / ground;
- hierarchy and salience;
- line, direction, balance, tension;
- crop, scale, perspective, depth;
- value and color relationships;
- type, image, evidence, and space interaction;
- sequence or workflow structure.

### 3. Interpretation

State the perception, behavior, emotion, meaning, or expectation created by those formal relationships.

Interpretation is a studio reading, not automatically the creator's intent or the ECD's authority.

### 4. Controlled transfer

Classify each useful finding as:

- transferable principle supporting the accepted project;
- authorized direct-use content;
- attribute open to current professional judgment;
- source-specific expression that must not transfer;
- evidence context that must remain unchanged;
- rights or similarity risk;
- issue requiring ECD alignment.

Controlled transfer must produce a project-specific solution. It cannot replace premise development, Core Communication Script, Editorial architecture, or Visual concept.

## Direct use and transformation

Before direct use or recognisable transformation, record:

- ownership and provenance;
- permission, license, and commercial-use status;
- allowed reproduction, crop, edit, quotation, or transformation;
- attribution and link requirements;
- brand, watermark, username, likeness, and identity conditions;
- evidence context that cannot be removed;
- publication, platform, region, and version limits.

When permission is unknown, the source may remain a Discovery Signal, inspiration, or research reference only. Do not invent permission.

## Public attribution rule

Do not automatically add a creator name, handle, source link, `灵感来源于`, or an infringement disclaimer merely because a public source contributed to discovery.

Public attribution is included when one or more conditions apply:

- the ECD explicitly requests it;
- the work quotes, reproduces, or recognisably transforms source material;
- source identity is material to the factual statement;
- platform, license, permission, or rights conditions require it;
- omission would make the public claim materially misleading.

If attribution is genuinely required but not authorized or resolvable, return the issue to Producer rather than silently adding or omitting it.

## Evidence references

A source assigned as Source / Evidence must retain the context necessary for correct interpretation.

Do not:

- crop away qualifications;
- restyle a reconstruction as documentary proof;
- present a creator's demonstration as the user's own result;
- use visual realism to imply verified operation;
- remove date, version, sample, region, or limitation when it changes the claim.

These controls apply only to factual dependencies and evidence. They do not require a source-facing explanation in an independently developed concept post that makes no such claim.

## Concept Reframing and independent expression

When `profiles/social-editorial/CONCEPT_REFRAMING.md` is active, the studio develops a new editorial and visual work rather than remaking the source.

Independent expression requires materially new:

- angle and proposition;
- communication progression;
- examples or scenarios;
- wording and rhetorical structure;
- visual concept, imagery, composition, and typography.

Similarity review asks:

- does the new work stand without the discovery source;
- are source-specific wording, examples, order, imagery, identity, and composition absent unless separately authorized;
- can the studio explain its own governing idea independently;
- would replacing the source leave the new work's editorial logic intact;
- do multiple sources remain distinguishable rather than collapsing into one imitation target.

This review remains backstage unless it creates a material publication decision.

## Creative transfer and similarity control

Transfer principles and relationships rather than source-specific expression.

A Quality Benchmark authorizes scrutiny of craft, not replication of composition or identity.

A Concept Reference authorizes development from an abstract relationship, not sentence-level paraphrase or structural copying.

A Composition Reference authorizes analysis of formal organization, not automatic reuse of the same subject placement and type arrangement.

## Stage responsibility

### Creative Producer

- preserves original ECD source intent;
- records source posture, authority class, roles, rights, publication dependency, stage projections, and activation;
- resolves cross-source or cross-department conflicts;
- ensures the ECD is not asked to restate the same source purpose without cause;
- prevents Discovery Signal from becoming an automatic public attribution or evidence obligation.

### Development Department

- determines Discovery Signal, factual dependency, direct-use, concept, rights, and boundary consequences;
- extracts an independently developable topic or concept;
- does not perform final visual extraction before Visual activation;
- includes only publication-relevant source meaning and exclusions in the Treatment.

### Core Communication Script and Editorial Department

- use only activated Editorial reference roles;
- build an independent progression and language;
- preserve evidence and attribution context only where material;
- do not derive page architecture from a discovery source merely because its original structure performed well;
- do not insert source or non-testing commentary absent an editorial need.

### Visual Department

- performs detailed Description → Formal Analysis → Interpretation → Controlled Transfer only for activated visual roles;
- develops original project-specific routes;
- preserves evidence and rights conditions;
- does not copy source-specific composition, characters, branded expression, or assets in Concept Reframing.

### Production Department

- uses only authorized assets, transformations, and accepted transfer instructions;
- does not introduce new source-derived expression for convenience;
- preserves required attribution, evidence, and source integrity in final assets;
- does not add unapproved source-facing labels or disclaimers.

## Multi-source conflicts

When sources disagree, record:

- roles assigned to each source;
- the dimension in conflict;
- which accepted project decision governs resolution;
- whether the conflict can be resolved professionally or requires ECD authority;
- which source-specific elements remain excluded.

Do not average conflicting sources into an incoherent style or editorial mixture.

## Stage-scoped activation

Source information may arrive at intake, but only the relevant role activates at each stage.

- Advisory may use Discovery Signal and engagement information.
- Development may activate source, evidence, concept, rights, and broad boundary roles.
- Core Communication Script / Editorial may activate factual, workflow, language, evidence, or negative-reference roles relevant to communication.
- Visual may activate mood, material, color, light, composition, typography, world, and benchmark roles after Creative Script Alignment.
- Production may activate direct-use assets and accepted implementation references after Art Direction authority.

Acceptance of a source's Discovery or Development role does not approve its later Editorial, Visual, or Production use.

## Quality gate

Reference governance passes when:

- original ECD intent and studio interpretation remain separate;
- every material source has an explicit posture and role;
- Discovery Signal is not mistaken for factual proof, direct-use permission, or a public attribution requirement;
- only activated roles affect the current stage;
- observation, analysis, interpretation, and authority are distinguishable where those passes are required;
- direct-use rights and attribution are recorded;
- evidence context remains accurate and inspectable;
- transferred principles guide original work rather than imitation;
- source-specific expression does not transfer without authorization;
- downstream owners receive both useful principles and prohibited uses;
- the final publication contains no unnecessary source or validation commentary.
