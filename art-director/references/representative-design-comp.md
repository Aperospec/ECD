# Representative Design Comp

## Purpose

The Representative Design Comp is Art Director's high-fidelity proof of the intended visual system before broad production.

It closes the gap between a low-fidelity Storyboard and a text-only Art Direction Package. Production Artist should not have to guess title scale, body-copy readability, image–type depth, grid behavior, or aesthetic finish from prose alone.

## What a Design Comp Is

A Design Comp uses:

- target aspect ratio and credible output geometry;
- real or accepted on-screen copy;
- intended typography hierarchy and line breaks;
- representative image assets or sufficiently faithful visual proxies;
- actual spacing, alignment, depth, masking, and image–type relationships;
- realistic content density;
- intended small-size behavior.

It is high fidelity in the dimensions that determine the visual premise. It need not contain final production polish in every asset.

## Required Coverage

Select the smallest set of screens that proves the system.

For a typical carousel, this normally includes:

1. **Cover / first frame** — proves feed impact, title scale, subject relationship, opening promise, and visual identity.
2. **Representative body page** — proves actual reading conditions, evidence or explanation treatment, hierarchy, and how the system works beyond the cover.

Add another Comp only when a materially different page class cannot be inferred, such as:

- evidence-heavy screenshot page;
- comparison;
- process diagram;
- dense data page;
- final payoff page with a distinct visual behavior.

Do not create every final page merely to call it a Comp.

## Cover Comp Tests

Confirm:

- the hero and opening promise are identifiable at feed-thumbnail size;
- the title has enough scale and confidence for the concept;
- overlap with the subject is intentional and preserves recognition;
- the subject retains volume, shading, perspective, and physical presence when required;
- no automatic text rectangle substitutes for composition;
- UI overlays and safe areas do not destroy the focal structure;
- the cover promise can be fulfilled by the Script.

## Body Comp Tests

Confirm:

- the primary message is discoverable immediately;
- supporting copy is readable at the intended mobile width;
- evidence remains large and credible enough to inspect;
- the page is not a reduced print-magazine page;
- hierarchy survives without depending on tiny decorative microtype;
- body grammar is related to the cover but not a repeated cover template;
- density leaves enough space for comprehension.

## Blur, Thumbnail, and Actual-Width Tests

Run:

- **Thumbnail test** — hero, primary line, and page meaning remain identifiable at feed preview size.
- **Blur / squint test** — dominant hierarchy remains obvious when detail is suppressed.
- **Actual-width test** — render at the approximate physical mobile display width or equivalent scaled preview; required text must be comfortably readable.

If the Comp fails, return to Art Director. Production scaling is not the place to repair a hierarchy that was already wrong.

## ECD Visual Alignment

Use a Comp for ECD alignment when it establishes a materially subjective visual premise, such as:

- mood, atmosphere, or visual world;
- durable visual identity;
- title–subject relationship;
- degree of restraint, tension, luxury, rawness, or experimentation;
- interpretation of a significant visual reference;
- the audience's emotional relationship to the work.

Art Director should present a recommendation, representative proof, and the material tradeoff. Do not provide many cosmetic variants without a real strategic difference.

## Lock Effect

An accepted or ECD-Aligned Comp may lock selected decisions for Production Artist:

- title scale relationship;
- typography personality;
- image–type depth behavior;
- grid / margin behavior;
- cover and body grammar;
- evidence treatment;
- small-size priorities;
- subject volume and contact behavior;
- permitted variables and tolerances.

The Comp does not lock provisional asset defects that Art Direction explicitly leaves open for production refinement.
